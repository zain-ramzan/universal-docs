# Privacy

UniversalDocs does not require an account. By default, its document bytes stay in the browser’s private storage or in the folder selected by the user. The application does not automatically upload documents, OCR text, video, or camera images.

Folder, camera, microphone, and operating-system sharing permissions are browser permissions. UniversalDocs requests them only after the person chooses a matching action. Private browser storage can be cleared by browser settings or storage pressure, so important private-storage documents should be exported as backups when that feature is available.

Sharing happens only when the user explicitly chooses a share or download action. The published site includes GoatCounter page-view analytics. UniversalDocs does not send document bytes, local filenames, extracted text, camera captures, or other document metadata to this analytics script; its purpose is to count visits to the public website. See [GoatCounter’s privacy information](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for its service-level details.
